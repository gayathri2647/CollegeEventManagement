# Team Responsibilities

## 1. Organizing Team
- Plan and coordinate the entire event.
- Manage event activities.
- Communicate with faculty coordinators.

## 2. Registration Team
- Register participants.
- Maintain participant details.
- Distribute registration IDs.

## 3. Cultural Team
- Organize dance and singing competitions.
- Coordinate performances.
- Manage stage activities.

## 4. Finance Team
- Prepare the event budget.
- Track expenses.
- Maintain payment records.

## 5. Hospitality Team
- Welcome guests.
- Arrange refreshments.
- Assist participants.

## 6. Technical Team
- Manage microphones and speakers.
- Operate projectors.
- Handle technical issues.

## 7. Social Media Team
- Create event posters.
- Publish announcements.
- Share event photographs and updates.

## Team Members

| Team | Team Leader | Responsibilities |
|---|---|---|
| Organizing | Student 1 | Overall coordination |
| Registration | Student 2 | Participant registration |
| Cultural | Student 3 | Competitions |
| Finance | Student 4 | Budget management |
| Hospitality | Student 5 | Guest management |

| Social Media | Student 7 | Promotions |
